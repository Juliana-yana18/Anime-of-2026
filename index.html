The History of Anime

The history of anime began in the early 20th century with Japanese animation experiments. The industry grew significantly in 1963 when Osamu Tezuka's Astro Boy helped establish the style of modern television anime.

Throughout the 1970s and 1980s, anime expanded rapidly, with popular genres such as mecha, fantasy, adventure, and science fiction. Studios such as Studio Ghibli also helped bring Japanese animation to international audiences.

Today, digital production and global streaming platforms have helped anime become a worldwide entertainment phenomenon enjoyed by millions of fans.
